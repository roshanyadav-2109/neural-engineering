Questions.

What does Neural Engineering do?

We build AI software for organisations. You tell us the problem. We design the product, write the code, launch it and keep it running.

Who is it for?

Companies, institutions and governments that want a real AI product in the hands of users, not a demo that never ships.

Do I need my own AI team?

No. We are the team. You bring the problem and someone who can make decisions.

What have you built?

Houdini, an AI operating system. Brain, enterprise AI from Neural AI. Divasya, a spiritual tech app. The Unknown IITians EdTech platform, Quiz Space and a student ERP. The PAN IIT summit app, ACRES dMRV for carbon credits, and enterprise EdTech for Wadhwani Foundation.

How do we start?

Email office@neuralai.in. We'll get on a call, understand the problem and come back with a plan.
